# Personal Edge

Results > Performance > Personal Edge describes the user's own settled paid or paper history. The two modes are separate. The panel loads only when opened and can be refreshed manually.

Verified leg decisions require an official final-stat source, `played` status, a numeric actual and line, and a Win/Loss result consistent with the recorded direction. Pushes, manual decisions, estimates, and conflicts are excluded from leg segments. A leg that wins on a losing card still counts as a winning leg. Card-size and grade summaries use settled card outcomes separately, including manually settled cards.

Segments cover sport, stat, provider, direction, stored confidence bucket, card size, and card grade. Each shows its sample count; fewer than 30 decisions is marked small. Highest/lowest observed segments are withheld until 30 verified decisions and are suppressed when the query's 5,000-placement limit truncates history. The oldest partial card is removed at that limit.

These are **tracked placements, not independent games**. Repeated legs can appear across cards, and historical associations do not establish causation or future profit. This panel does not change recommendation scores or paid-entry eligibility.
