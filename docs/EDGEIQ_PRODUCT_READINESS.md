## Blockers Before Public Beta

- Complete the consumer acceptance matrix in EDGEIQ_CONSUMER_PRODUCT_UPGRADE.md.
- Verify hosted sessions, preference ownership and feedback attribution.
- Verify PostgreSQL upgrade/rollback, backup restore and scheduler execution.
- Resolve Railway routing and verify the deployed branch/version.
- Validate ledger/payout behavior and unresolved-state recovery across providers.
- Verify mobile overflow, keyboard access, loading, empty and error states.
- Keep unsupported evidence research-only with small-sample warnings.

## Blockers Before Monetization

- All public-beta blockers and measured usability/reliability acceptance.
- Provider commercial rights and dependable payout/settlement coverage.
- Privacy, responsible-use and consumer disclosures review.
- Independently settled track record without misleading accuracy or profit claims.
- Support, incident response, recovery and account lifecycle readiness.
- Billing is intentionally excluded from this reset.
